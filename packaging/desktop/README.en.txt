LiteSic for Windows

Extract the entire ZIP into a writable folder and open LiteSic.exe.
Keep locales, resources and licenses next to the executable.
No development tools or additional runtimes are required.

Standard edition: settings and playlist use %LOCALAPPDATA%\LiteSic.
Portable edition: resources\portable.flag enables portable mode on double
click. Settings and playlist use data\ next to the executable. Keep that
marker to remain portable. The standard edition also supports --portable.

Click LIST, ADD and select files/folders, or drag music from Explorer.
Double-click a track to play. EQ opens the equalizer; VIZ toggles the
visualizer. Right-click the player for themes, language, scale and hotkeys.

MP3, WAV, FLAC and OGG Vorbis; M3U/M3U8 playlists; favorites; 18 languages.
To export, select General or Favorites, then LIST > Save playlist.
General saves all tracks; Favorites saves only starred tracks. Search and
row selection do not limit exports. Choose M3U or M3U8 in the Save dialog;
both formats preserve favorite flags when reopened in LiteSic.
Playlists store absolute media paths. If you move your music to another
folder or drive, add it again or update the playlist.
Targets Windows 7-11, x86/x64. Tested on Windows 11; practical validation
on Windows 7/8/8.1/10 remains pending.
